## Hands-On Cybersecurity Learning Platforms

### Hack Smarter Labs
Website: https://hacksmarter.org/

Hack Smarter offers labs and courses covering Active Directory, web application security, Windows, Linux, and AWS. Most labs are multi-machine, and full network environments are planned for release in February 2026. Subscriptions start at $6.99 per month, and the platform also offers free and pay-what-you-can training starting at $0.

### Hack The Box
Website: https://app.hackthebox.com/

Hack The Box is an extremely comprehensive platform with a massive amount of content spanning both red team and blue team disciplines. It covers nearly every major area of offensive and defensive security and includes a substantial amount of completely free material. Subscriptions start at $17 per month for the main platform and $40 per month for the Academy. Current students can receive significant discounts on the Academy subscription.

### HackingHub
Website: https://hackinghub.com/

HackingHub is almost entirely free and provides a wide range of challenges and longer missions designed to simulate real-world web application vulnerabilities. All labs on the platform are free to use. For learners looking to go deeper, the platform also offers paid courses taught by Adam Langley, Ben Sadeghipour, and John Hammond.

### Pwned Labs
Website: https://pwnedlabs.io/

Pwned Labs is a cloud-focused platform covering both red team and blue team techniques. It offers highly realistic labs centered around AWS, Azure, GCP, and Oracle Cloud environments. The platform includes a fair amount of free content, with full access available through a subscription that starts at approximately $16 per month when billed annually.

### Cybr
Website: https://cybr.com/

Cybr is widely regarded as one of the strongest platforms for learning AWS pentesting and cloud blue teaming. It provides in-depth video courses covering a wide range of AWS security topics, as well as content on web application pentesting. New material is frequently added by Christophe Limpalair. Some content is free, while full access to all labs and courses starts at approximately $16 per month with an annual subscription.

### PortSwigger Academy
Website: https://portswigger.net/

PortSwigger Academy is often overlooked, but it is one of the best resources available for learning web application pentesting and is completely free. While a small number of labs require a Burp Suite Pro license, the vast majority of labs can be completed without any subscription.
